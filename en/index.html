Ask your own question
Who issues the medical certificate?

A medical institution issues the certificate based on a doctors' examination. We advise on the requirements and help you book the visit.

How do I book a consultation?

Send a request on the site, call us or message us. A manager will confirm the details and offer options.

Which documents should I bring?

It depends on the certificate. An identity document is usually required. The manager will give you the exact list for your service during the consultation.

Can I book for today?

That depends on how busy the clinic is. Add your preferred date to the request and the manager will suggest the nearest available slot.

How much does it cost?

The price depends on the certificate or check-up. Indicative prices are on the service pages; the manager confirms the final amount before booking.
