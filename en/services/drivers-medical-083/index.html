About the service

Who needs certificate 083/o

Medical certificate form 083/o confirms that a person is medically fit to drive. Without it, a driving school or a Ministry of Internal Affairs service centre will not accept your documents.

The certificate is issued by a medical institution after a doctors' examination. The institution decides who sits on the board and which tests are needed: usually a general practitioner, ophthalmologist, ENT specialist, neurologist and surgeon. Narcologist and psychiatrist certificates are required separately.

How the medical board works

At the clinic you see each doctor on the board in turn. Each records a conclusion, and the head of the board then makes the overall decision on fitness to drive.

How long the visit takes depends on how busy the clinic is and whether you already hold valid narcologist and psychiatrist certificates. If a doctor orders an additional test, the certificate is issued once its results are in.

How to prepare

  • Bring your glasses or contact lenses if you use them
  • Do not drink alcohol the day before the examination
  • Tell the doctor about chronic conditions and any medication you take
